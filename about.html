Our Story

Why PEXXORAA exists

PEXXORAA started with a simple observation: most small businesses were choosing between two bad options — a cheap, templated website that undersold their work, or an agency that treated them like a low priority. We built PEXXORAA to be the third option: senior-level creative and engineering, delivered with the speed and care that growing businesses actually need.

Today we work with startups, clinics, restaurants, schools, and personal brands across a wide range of industries — but every project still starts with the same question: what does this business need to be taken seriously online?
